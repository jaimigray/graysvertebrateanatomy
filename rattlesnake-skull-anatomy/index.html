Colors of Skull Anatomy

Mexican Small Headed Rattlesnake Skull Anatomy

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom.

Skull of a Mexican small headed rattlesnake, Crotalus intermedius, with bones colored and labelled to demonstrate skull anatomy. This 3D model was produced from a CT scan of a Florida Museum of Natural History specimen (UF:herp:52552). Segmentation and mesh files generated using 3D Slicer. Meshes further edited in Blender. Anatomy follows Cundall and Irish (2008) “The Snake Skull” in Biology of the Reptilia, Volume 20, Morphology H (Gans, Gaunt and Adler). Image credit: "mexican smallhead rattlesnake, viborilla" by Wouter Beukema is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
