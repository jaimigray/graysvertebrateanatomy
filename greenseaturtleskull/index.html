Colors of Skull Anatomy

Green Sea Turtle Skull Anatomy

Skull of a green sea turtle, Chelonia mydas, with bones colored and labelled to demonstrate skull anatomy. Digital dissection and scene by Jaimi Gray (oVert team). 3D model produced from a CT scan of a Florida Museum of Natural History specimen (UF:herp:51412, head only specimen). Segmentation and mesh files generated using VGStudio Max. Meshes further edited in Blender.
